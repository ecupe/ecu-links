About the studio

MECU Interactive is an independent video game development studio founded by Mateo Amendano.

Our vision is to combine creativity and technology to create game experiences with a character of their own. We are a new studio, and Wanderbud is the game we are building right now. Announcements, screenshots, and progress will be posted here and on Instagram.

  1. 01

    Play comes first

    If it is not fun to pick up and play, it is not ready. Everything else is built around that.

  2. 02

    Craft over shortcuts

    We would rather make one thing well than many things quickly.

  3. 03

    Open about the work

    We share progress as it happens, including the parts that are still rough.
